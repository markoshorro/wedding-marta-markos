Frequently Asked Questions

What will the weather be like?

Galicia is our home, and we love it, but maybe its weather isn't the best in Spain. Said this, during summer it should be fine, even reaching 30ºC, but it could also rain. You never know. Be prepared for a warm temperature, but also for the rain.

What is the dress code?

Anything is valid, but light colors (because you don't want to eclipse the bride, right?).

Will transportation be arranged?

Yes, from Cambados and Vilagarcía de Arousa.
